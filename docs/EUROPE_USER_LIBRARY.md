# Europe library (user curated)

The built-in Europe preset uses the owner's `street-clues-Europe.json` as its starting point. It contains 38 country candidates and 115 observations: 42 original image cards, kept with their image bytes and stable IDs, plus 38 ccTLD cards, 33 readable-language cards and two driving-side cards. The rebuilt JSON is in `public/libraries/europe.json`. The owner's importable export is `street-clues-Europe-tidied.json` (kept local to avoid duplicate publication). Country-scope labels were tidied in English and Chinese.

The UI and custom-library engine use a uniform 38-candidate prior. With one target of weight L and 37 unspecified targets at 1, a match share of 90% requires L≈333. We calibrated estimated **seen likelihood ratios**, not direct percentage boosts: a readable country-code domain uses 800; a distinctive single-country language uses up to 450; distinctive image metas generally use 70–280; generic vegetation and scenery use about 1.5–12. Shared languages and road-side observations keep all applicable countries in play. Every explicit-absence multiplier remains 1 until separate absence evidence exists. Unknown countries are neutral rather than treated as negative evidence. These values are engineering estimates and not measured GeoGuessr image frequencies.

The owner's three limited-area annotations remain visible in the corresponding labels: the Moscow-area trees, winter Gen 4 imagery in western Lithuania and rooftop tanks in Asian Turkey. Their country-level multipliers are reduced. This library contains no Europe region scheme, so those notes do not produce city or provincial percentages. Other language variants (for example Belgian French and Finnish Swedish) receive lower country weights where use is regional. Source data for the new country-code cards was checked against [IANA's root zone](https://www.iana.org/domains/root/db); language and driving-side context was checked against [EU language information](https://translation.ec.europa.eu/languages-eu-why-multilingualism-matters/linguistic-diversity-eu_en) and [European road rules](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/travel/driving-abroad/road-rules-and-safety/index_en.htm). The owner's original photographs and specific image-meta claims have not been independently frequency measured.
