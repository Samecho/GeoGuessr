# Built-in Africa library

Reviewed 2026-09-26. The read-only Africa preset is `public/libraries/africa.json` and its manifest is `src/data/africa-library-manifest.json`. It was promoted from the reviewed personal-library export `street-clues-custom-library-africa-road-prefixes.json` after importer validation and browser checks. It contains 94 bilingual clues: 56 with embedded images and 38 text-only. Images stay embedded in the static JSON and are loaded only when the user selects this library. The Global library and its separate scoring data are not modified.

The preset covers exactly 14 documented candidates: Botswana, Eswatini, Ghana, Kenya, Lesotho, Namibia, Nigeria, Réunion, Rwanda, São Tomé and Príncipe, Senegal, South Africa, Tunisia, and Uganda. This is the scope of the supplied JSON, not a claim that every African country is fully researched. A country's absence from this preset is not evidence against it in the Global library. Manual scope intersects this list without adding countries or scores.

The source material for the pre-existing African clue cards came from the owner's local `tuxundoc/非洲/*/index.html` tutorial archive and associated images. The author permission reported by the owner applies to this project; the code's MIT license does not cover the tutorial images. The image audit is [here](AFRICA_PLATE_IMAGES.md). Twelve text-only road-number prefixes were added from local chapters and cited road authority/government documents; the source and per-prefix multiplier review is [here](AFRICA_ROAD_PREFIXES.md). The personal JSON did not carry a per-clue source field; this preset therefore retains the available chapter-level provenance and does not claim that every multiplier is a measured frequency or independently fact-checked statistic.

The Africa preset uses the same versioned `CustomLibrary` JSON parser and likelihood-multiplier engine as personal libraries, but it is bundled and read-only. This is a deliberate data-engine distinction from the original Global library's source-fact evidence model. The two libraries never score one another's clues. The app loads this JSON on demand from its own static site; there is no external API call. `npm run data:validate` checks its schema, image count, candidate list and weight targets on every build.

## 2026-09-27 road-marking calibration

A report with right-side traffic, a white centerline, and a green-tinted plate previously ranked Tunisia first (30.0%). The broad white-centerline card was the cause: it gave Tunisia an 8× multiplier, Ghana and Senegal 5×, and Réunion 4×. These were estimates, not observed frequencies. The local chapters describe white road lines in Tunisia and Ghana and a white centerline shared by southern African countries; they do not establish that one white centerline is a rare, country-level identifier. Relevant passages are in `tuxundoc/非洲/tunisia/index.html` (road lines), `tuxundoc/非洲/ghana/index.html` (road markings), and the Botswana, Eswatini, Lesotho, Namibia, and South Africa chapters (southern marking pattern).

The standalone white-centerline estimate is now 1.4× for the five documented southern African countries and 1.15× for Tunisia and Ghana. Senegal and Réunion have no added multiplier from this clue because the cited material did not verify the centerline specifically. Unlisted countries remain unknown/neutral at 1×. These values are deliberately weak initial estimates, not empirical occurrence rates. The green-tinted plate clue and right-side traffic clue were not downweighted. With the reported three observations, the corrected ranking places Nigeria above Tunisia; the test in `src/custom/africa-ranking.test.ts` protects that relationship and the weak standalone line effect.

## 2026-09-27 plate-card consolidation

After the 2026-09-27 source-image audit, the plate section has 22 cards: five broad color observations with parallel bilingual wording (white, yellow, green, blue, black) and seventeen visually distinct detailed observations. Four formerly separate green/blue descriptions and an overlapping white/green/yellow card were merged. Broad color observations are text-only; images appear only on the sixteen detailed plate layouts, whose labels specify the visible design. Detailed cards, including front-white/rear-yellow on the same vehicle, replace their broad color evidence when both are selected. The full local tutorial archive is unchanged.

The green card keeps Nigeria, Eswatini, South Africa, and South Africa's Free State as positive targets. It gives modest counter-evidence only to Ghana, Senegal, and Tunisia, whose local chapters explicitly describe usual non-green plate designs: `tuxundoc/非洲/ghana/index.html` contrasts Ghana's usual white/yellow plates with green-tinted Nigerian plates; `tuxundoc/非洲/senegal/index.html` describes solid blue plates and the newer white plate with a blue band; `tuxundoc/非洲/tunisia/index.html` describes black plates with white characters. The South Africa chapter specifically notes green-tinted Free State and blue-tinted KwaZulu-Natal plates; those regional relationships remain in the merged cards. These counter-evidence multipliers are fallible initial estimates (0.65×, 0.7×, and 0.5× respectively), not claims that a green plate is impossible there. Other unmentioned countries, including Réunion, stay neutral at 1×.

## 2026-09-27 broad plate presentation and regional normalization

The five broad observations describe only whether white, yellow, green, blue, or black is visible anywhere on a plate. They are text cards. All seventeen plate image cards describe a specific visible layout, using their existing reviewed tutorial images. No image was removed from the detailed cards. The entire local source archive remains untouched.

The green and blue broad cards have country multipliers and South African provincial multipliers from the local South Africa chapter. Their provincial values now carry `regionalWeightMode: "conditional"`: a province multiplier redistributes that observation within South Africa, while its mean is normalized out of the South Africa country likelihood. Thus the 25× Free State green value no longer inflates a green-only country result above the 4× Nigeria country value; Free State still leads the conditional South Africa region chart. The same correction applies to KwaZulu-Natal on the blue broad card. These are estimated multipliers, not measured frequencies.

## 2026-09-28 Rwanda languages

Added bilingual text cards for Kinyarwanda (120× Rwanda) and Kiswahili (12× Kenya, 3× Uganda, 2× Rwanda), and modest Rwanda support on the existing English (5×) and French (4×) cards. These are provisional likelihood multipliers for actually visible text, not measured sign frequencies or claims of exclusivity. The four official languages are documented by the [Government of Rwanda](https://www.gov.rw/about); Kenya’s [constitution](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/act/2010/constitution/eng%402010-09-03/source) and [Uganda’s curriculum authority](https://ncdc.go.ug/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/Terminology-Development-Handbook-Jan-2025_BOOK-WEB-FILE-1.pdf) establish that Kiswahili is shared. The Africa preset now has 109 clues: 69 pictured and 40 text-only.
