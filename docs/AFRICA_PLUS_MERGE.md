# Africa Plus merge review

Reviewed 2026-09-27. The owner supplied `C:/Users/samecho/Downloads/street-clues-Africa.json` as a 16-clue personal library. The original file was read, not changed. Its twelve embedded images were visually compared with their labels and retained byte-for-byte in the built-in `public/libraries/africa.json`. Four text-only notes were folded into existing observations instead of appearing as duplicate cards. The official preset went from 95 to **107 distinct clues**, from 57 to **69 pictured clues**, and from 65 to **77 image instances**; the number of text-only clues stays **38**. The candidate list remains the existing 14 documented countries/covered areas.

The supplied JSON has no per-image author, original URL, or license field. Image provenance for these twelve is therefore the specific clue ID in the owner's original JSON, not an invented website credit. The owner reported permission for the project; these image rights are distinct from the code license. This review does not turn the photos into a frequency sample. Individual existing externally sourced plate-photo credits remain in the library.

All multipliers below are **comparative initial estimates**, never measured occurrence probabilities. Unlisted countries stay neutral (1×), not negative. Every added clue has a separate clearly-absent multiplier of 1× because the supplied photos and cited guides do not establish reliable rates of absence across map generations or scenes. Where a photo alone shows an object but no guide establishes its geographic distribution, its multiplier is deliberately small pending further review. These estimates affect only the Africa preset, not the Global library.
